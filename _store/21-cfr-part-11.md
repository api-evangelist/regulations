description: 21 CFR Part 11 is the FDA rule setting the conditions under which electronic records and
  electronic signatures are treated as equivalent to paper and ink. It requires validated systems, secure
  computer-generated time-stamped audit trails that record who changed what and when without obscuring
  the prior value, operational and authority checks, and the ability to produce accurate copies for inspection.
  Any API writing to a record that supports an FDA submission inherits these obligations.
