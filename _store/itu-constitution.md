The ITU is the oldest institution in this catalog and operates a layer above national regulators, allocating
the resources that make international communications possible at all.

  * **Radio Regulations** - A binding treaty instrument allocating spectrum and satellite orbital positions
    between states.
  * **Numbering and identification** - E.164 international numbering, the plan behind every phone number a
    network API verifies.
  * **ITU-T Recommendations** - Technical standards underpinning interoperability across national networks.
  * **Treaty-based, not developer-facing** - The ITU regulates states and administrations rather than platforms,
    and mandates nothing about API exposure.

E.164 deserves a note for anyone building against network APIs: it is the identifier every CAMARA number
verification and SIM swap call is keyed on, and it is an ITU artifact. *The State of Telecom APIs* also recorded
an honest caveat about the ITU's presence in the API Evangelist catalog — the two machine-readable definitions
attributed to it were reconstructed during enrichment, and the ITU has not published, reviewed or endorsed
them.
