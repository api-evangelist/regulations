Ofcom's remit is broad and its API relevance is entirely indirect, which is itself the finding.

  * **Converged regulation** - Telecoms, broadcast, post and spectrum under a single regulator.
  * **Competition and access** - Significant market power remedies and wholesale access obligations shape
    carrier-to-carrier terms rather than developer-facing ones.
  * **Consumer protection** - Switching, contract terms and complaint handling.
  * **No API mandate** - Nothing requires network capability to be exposed as a contract.

*The State of Telecom APIs* found the UK the cleanest example of what voluntary standardisation produces: BT/EE,
Virgin Media O2, Three and Vodafone all launched CAMARA KYC Age Verification and Tenure commercially on the same
day in September 2025, reachable through Jersey Telecom and TMT ID — and not one of the four exposes an endpoint of
its own. Four regulated operators, one coordinated launch, zero first-party developer surfaces.
