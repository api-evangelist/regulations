The **Retail Payment Activities Act (RPAA)** brings Canada's payment service providers under the supervision of the Bank of Canada, requiring them to register and to meet operational-risk and end-user-fund-safeguarding standards. It is the supervisory half of Canada's payments modernization.

  * **Supervision of PSPs** - Payment service providers must register with, and are overseen by, the Bank of Canada, with requirements around risk management and safeguarding customer funds.
  * **Part of payments modernization** - The RPAA travels with Payments Canada's Real-Time Rail and Lynx as the reform of how money moves in Canada.
  * **Infrastructure backdrop to open banking** - It governs the rails that, in my Canadian banking research, out-score the banks themselves.

The RPAA matters to the API story because in Canada the API surface that exists is the *rails*, not the banks — and the rails are what this act supervises. It is the regulation behind the payment infrastructure that, absent a live open-banking mandate, is where Canadian financial-API value actually sits today.
