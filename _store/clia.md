kind: statute
jurisdiction: United States
yearCreated: 1988
description: CLIA establishes federal quality standards for all laboratory testing performed on human
  specimens for diagnosis, prevention or treatment. Administered by CMS, it certifies laboratories by
  test complexity and governs personnel qualifications, proficiency testing, quality control and result
  reporting. A genomics or diagnostics API returning a clinical result is returning the output of a CLIA-certified
  process, and the certification travels with the result.
